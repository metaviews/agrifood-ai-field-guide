## Quote / Position

> "I no longer go to work like when I was 20 years old. My body feels more and more tired. I'm not going to be able to do the same [work], I'm going to have to dedicate myself to other tasks."

## Context

The quote opens the CHI 2024 study and grounds the technology discussion in a worker's body, health, age, and future work options. It should be paired with the study's finding that workers want safer, more ergonomic environments and technical skill development opportunities.

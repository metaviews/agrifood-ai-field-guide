## Quote / Position

> "When they tried a machine that cut the lettuce, the lettuce was cut upwards and they tore it apart, the one with hearts [romaine]... Then I had to fix it. So, people are always going to be needed."

## Context

This farmworker focus-group quote is from Sullivan SSRC Just Tech September 2025 essay — one of the rare verbatim farmworker voices in the agrifood AI corpus. The quote captures the technology-readiness limit of automated romaine lettuce harvest (the machine tears the lettuce rather than cutting it cleanly) and the farmworker's structural insight that *people are always going to be needed* — the labour-displacement-doesn't-happen-yet framing for harvest automation specifically.

Worth knowing because:
- The quote is a *farmworker voice* on agrifood AI deployment, not a vendor or analyst voice. The Sullivan unit names the substantive US critical-voice contribution as centred on farmworker experience; this quote is the verbatim anchor.
- The quote is from a *focus group*, not an interview — meaning it is a *collective* farmworker perspective on automation, not an individual voice.
- The quote is dated December 2025 (focus group conducted) — *current* as of mid-2026.

This is the first verbatim farmworker quote in the field guide. It builds the `quotes/farmworkers/` category that the Sullivan unit names but the corpus previously did not have.

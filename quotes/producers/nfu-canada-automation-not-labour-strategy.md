## Quote / Position

> "We reject a view that seeks to reduce labour costs by maximizing the use of technology (robots, automation, self-driving tractors, etc.) and adopting a ‘just-in-time’ approach to the employment of Temporary Foreign Workers."

## Context

This is the clearest primary-source NFU statement for the AI-and-labour spine. It should not be presented as a blanket rejection of agricultural technology. The NFU's position is that automation cannot substitute for a labour strategy grounded in fair compensation, safe and dignified work, worker rights, farm viability, and food-system resilience.

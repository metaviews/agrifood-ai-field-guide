## Quote / Position

> "You need hands to cut lettuce. Think about a robot with a knife. That's not happening... It's just so delicate. It's a really hard nut to crack."

## Context

This roboticist interview quote is from Sullivan SSRC Just Tech September 2025 essay — the *roboticist* counterpart to the farmworker focus-group quote. The quote captures the structural difficulty of automating head-lettuce harvest from the *robotics engineering* side: hands + knife + delicate vegetable + within-second skill selection = *a really hard nut to crack*.

Worth knowing because:
- The quote is a *robotics-engineering* voice on agrifood AI deployment, not a vendor marketing voice. The roboticist is *insider* to the technology; the *honest* framing of the difficulty is structurally distinctive from the investor-pitch "harvest automation is the holy grail" framing.
- The quote pairs with the Sullivan farmworker focus-group quote as a *dual-insider* framing: farmworker + roboticist both name harvest automation as not-yet-achievable at commercial scale.
- The quote is dated February 2025 (interview conducted) — *current* as of mid-2026.

Together with the farmworker focus-group quote, this roboticist quote is the substantive dual-insider voice on the harvest-vs-weeding split that the Sullivan unit names as the structural finding. The vendor and investor pitches conflate progress on weeding with progress on harvest; the farmworker + roboticist voices name the harvest-as-not-solvable framing from the *insider* position.

## Quote / Position

> "Performativity and Non-Disruptive Disruption in Silicon Valley" — the structural framing of agrifood tech venture capital pitch as *non-disruptive disruption*. Agrifood tech investor claims of disruption without the structural labour-displacement that disruption would imply; the pitch structure is performative (the disruption is the pitch, not the deployment).

## Context

This paraphrased-position entry is the UCSC critical-food-systems cluster's substantive analytical framing of agrifood tech investment. The *Pitching Agri-Food Tech* paper (Fairbairn, Kish, Guthman, *Journal of Cultural Economy* 15(5), 2022) is the *structural critique* of agritech investment framing.

Worth knowing because:
- The framing names the *structural pattern* of agrifood tech venture capital as *non-disruptive disruption*: investor claims of disruption (yield improvement, labour displacement, scale) without the actual deployment that would deliver the disruption. The pitch is performative; the deployment is partial.
- The framing is *substantive* analytical contribution: it engages the venture-capital structure of agrifood AI investment, not the technology itself.
- The framing is *complementary* to the Sullivan ethnography: Sullivan documents the *deployment-reality gap* (weeding commercial, harvest not); Fairbairn + Kish + Guthman document the *pitch-reality gap* (pitch-disruption, deployment-partial).

Together the UCSC cluster's analytical position (Sullivan ethnography + Fairbairn + Kish + Guthman *Pitching Agri-Food Tech* + Guthman *Problem with Solutions* UC Press 2024 + Mitchell *Taylorism Comes to the Fields* 2023 + Friedland et al. *Manufacturing Green Gold* 1981) forms the corpus's substantive US critical-voice analytical cluster for agrifood AI and labour.

## Quote / Position

> "The CLC believes that transparency and public consultation are essential and indispensable elements of an effective and adequate regulatory framework for the development and application of AI."

## Context

This quote gives the Canadian labour-centre layer a direct governance test: transparency and consultation are not optional communications practices, but conditions of adequate AI regulation. It pairs with UFCW Canada's workplace-level emphasis on worker consultation and employer transparency.

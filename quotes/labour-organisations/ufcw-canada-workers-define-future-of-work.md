## Quote / Position

> "The new world of work must not be imposed. Workers must help to define it and control it. The new world of work must mean good jobs."

## Context

This is a concise worker-governance anchor for the AI-and-labour archetype. It frames worker voice as a condition of legitimate technological change, not as a post-deployment consultation exercise.

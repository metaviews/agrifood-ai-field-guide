## Quote / Position

> "Our Local Unions strive to ensure that members are consulted on the introduction of new technologies, and that employers are transparent about the impacts these technologies might have on jobs."

## Context

This is the most directly operational UFCW Canada statement for workplace AI governance. It gives the labour-side test for a deployment: were workers consulted, and did the employer disclose likely job impacts? It should be paired with the unit's discussion of automated performance tracking and algorithmic-management risk.

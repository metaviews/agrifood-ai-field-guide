### Maple Leaf Foods (London ON + Heritage Plant)

Per `units/maple-leaf-foods-ai-canada.md`:
- **Deployment scale**:
  - London ON poultry plant (660,000 sq ft; Maple Leaf's "world's largest poultry plant" framing)
  - Heritage Plant (processed meats)
- **AI deployment**:
  - Emulate3D digital twin + ML (>99% material-handling accuracy in grading + packaging area)
  - Braincube Real-Time Process Optimization (+12% yield / 3-month ROI / 100% golden batches)
  - AVEVA MES
- **Labour-side framing** named in unit: *"The technology has knock-on implications for worker-safety and ergonomics improvements that don't get AI-tagged in trade press."*
- **Labour-monitoring dual-use** named in global cycle's NA meat-processing unit (Cargill CarVe + Tyson × AWS + Smithfield); **Maple Leaf Foods + Emulate3D digital twin + Braincube has the same dual-use structure but the labour-monitoring dimension is *not named* in the unit.**
- **UFCW representation** — Maple Leaf Foods workers are UFCW-organised; specific collective bargaining terms on AI deployment not surfaced (G-388).

### Saputo + Olymel + Agropur (SoraLINK predictive maintenance)

Per `units/soralink-export-food-processing.md`:
- **Deployment scale**: SoraLINK predictive maintenance AI deployed across Saputo + Olymel + Agropur (Canada's three largest food processors).
  - **Saputo** — dairy, international operations in US, Australia, Argentina, UK
  - **Olymel** — largest Canadian pork and poultry processor
  - **Agropur** — largest Canadian dairy cooperative
- **Vendor-reported ROI**: $250,000 saved in a single intervention (vendor framing).
- **Labour-side framing**: predictive maintenance AI is *primarily* yield-improving + worker-safety (reduces unplanned downtime; reduces worker exposure to emergency-repair scenarios); *secondarily* labour-monitoring (the predictive maintenance layer is less labour-monitoring-heavy than line-side CV).
- **UFCW representation** — Saputo + Olymel + Agropur workers are UFCW-organised; specific collective bargaining terms on AI deployment not surfaced (G-389).

### CAAIN meat processing portfolio (P&P Optica + mode40 + Circulus)

Per `units/canadian-meat-processing-ai.md`:
- **P&P Optica (Waterloo ON)** — hyperspectral imaging + ML for meat quality assessment; ~20 systems installed across pork and poultry operations in North America; founders Romek + Olga Pawluczyk; pivoted to meat in 2015.
- **mode40 (Steinbach MB)** — AI carcass cooling (Meat Quality Management system); 40-person cross-functional engineering team; 2 → 5 → 8 → planned 14 hire pattern supported by CAAIN funding; 10-plant validation in progress.
- **Circulus Agtech (Montréal QC)** — modular on-farm manure treatment system; closed-loop nutrient recovery (biological stabilization + solid-liquid separation + membrane filtration); methane reduction + biofertilizer production.

Total Canadian meat processing AI deployment: **$11.89M+ in CAAIN-funded project value across 3 projects, $3.94M CAAIN contribution**.

### Canadian dairy AI portfolio (CATTLEytics + SomaDetect + Milk Moovement + Lakeland)

Per `units/canadian-dairy-ai.md`:
- **CATTLytics (Hamilton ON)** — equipment-agnostic dairy platform; digital twinning + predictive modelling + AI chat interface; DVM + McMaster Computer Engineering + Fine Arts minor; CEO Dr. Shari Van de Pol; 2014 spin-off of Feedlot Health Management Services (acquired by TELUS Agriculture); $800K CAAIN / $2.44M total.
- **SomaDetect (Tillsonburg ON)** — light-scattering + ML for cow-specific real-time milk analysis; TRL 8+; subclinical ketosis + Energy-Corrected Milk (ECM); Walker Dairy partner; $145K CAAIN / $428K total.
- **Milk Moovement (Halifax NS)** — cloud-based dairy supply-chain software; $20M USD Series A (2022) led by VMG Catalyst; AI tool "Moove" ML + optimization algorithms for raw-milk transportation.
- **Lakeland College (Vermilion AB)** — Precision Ranching rumen bolus + GPS ear tags + ultrasound + LlamaZOO digital twin; spans AB + MB + BC ranches; $143K CAAIN / $1.30M total; completed December 2024.

**Distinctive feature**: CATTLEytics + SomaDetect both emphasise *"equipment-agnostic"* framing — distinct from vendor-locked systems. The equipment-agnostic framing is *labour-friendly* (farmers retain data control; vendor lock-in is the labour-side counter).

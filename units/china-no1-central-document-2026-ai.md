## Content

《中共中央 国务院关于锚定农业农村现代化 扎实推进乡村全面振兴的意见》, issued **3 January 2026** and published 4 February 2026 — the **2026 No. 1 Central Document**, the first of the **15th Five-Year Plan (十五五, 2026–2030)** era and the annual instrument that sets binding rural work priorities for ministries and provinces.

**The AI clause, in full context.** The document does not carry a standalone AI section. AI appears in Article (四) 提升农业科技创新效能 (improving the effectiveness of agricultural science and technology innovation), in a sentence about **农业新质生产力 (new quality productive forces in agriculture)**:

> "因地制宜发展农业新质生产力，促进人工智能与农业发展相结合，拓展无人机、物联网、机器人等应用场景" — *"develop agricultural new-quality productive forces in line with local conditions, **promote the integration of artificial intelligence with agricultural development, and expand application scenarios for drones, the internet of things and robots**."*

The surrounding article assigns: R&D and application of **high-end intelligent and hilly-mountain-suitable agricultural machinery**; continued implementation of the **seed-industry revitalisation action (种业振兴行动)** with **industrialisation of bio-breeding (推进生物育种产业化)**; reform of agricultural research institutes; reform of the grass-roots agricultural extension system (基层农技推广体系) so that results "enter villages and households"; and reform of agricultural higher education toward a "new agricultural science" (新农科) with directed training of agricultural talent.

**Other articles that bear on AI deployment.**

- **Grain floor:** output "stabilised at around **1.4 trillion jin**" (~700 Mt) with a new round of the 千亿斤 grain-capacity programme, integrated gains from good land, good seed, good machinery and good methods.
- **Subsidy integrity:** the machinery purchase and application subsidy (农机购置与应用补贴) is to be implemented on a "优机优补" (subsidise good machinery) basis with **whole-chain regularised supervision (全链条常态化监管)**.
- **Trade:** diversified agricultural imports; support for expanding exports of competitive specialty products; participation in international food and agriculture governance.
- **Structural adjustment:** hog capacity management; consolidation of beef and dairy industry relief; support for facility-agriculture renovation; **deep-sea aquaculture and modern distant-water fishing**; and "biological agriculture".
- **Risk:** agricultural insurance and disaster prevention with stronger monitoring and forecasting systems.

## What this unit is doing in the taxonomy

The **annual-binding-instrument unit** for China. Where `china-smart-agriculture-action-plan-2024-2028.md` is a ministry plan with named products and numeric targets, the No. 1 Document is the political priority signal that makes those plans fundable. Together they are the two-document chain that explains how AI reaches Chinese farms: an annual political mandate plus a five-year ministry implementation plan.

## Why it matters for talks

- **The grammar is the finding.** In Chinese rural policy AI is not framed as a technology sector or a governance problem; it is framed as one component of **new quality productive forces**, listed alongside drones, IoT and robots. Any talk comparing national AI-in-agriculture approaches should lead with this difference in framing rather than with deployment counts.
- **The explicit scenario list — drones, IoT, robots — is the state's own account of where AI means something in agriculture.** It matches the corpus's deployment-heavy China units and omits the analytical/decision-support layer entirely.
- **"优机优补" plus whole-chain supervision** is the answer to subsidy fraud, and worth pairing with the subsidy-integrity evidence in the machinery units.
- **Bio-breeding industrialisation sits in the same article as AI**, which links this document to the corpus's seed and plant-breeding units: in Chinese policy, AI and gene editing are the same productivity agenda.

## Critical context

- **One clause, no budget.** The AI sentence carries no figure, no programme name and no deadline. It is an authorisation, not a commitment, and any claim that China has "budgeted for AI in agriculture" needs a different source.
- **"New quality productive forces" (新质生产力) is a political-economic term** introduced at the top of the Chinese policy system; its appearance here tells you the priority level, not the funding level.
- **Extension reform is named as necessary.** The document requires reform of the grass-roots extension system so results reach villages — an admission that the last mile is the weak link in Chinese agricultural technology translation, and the closest Chinese analogue to the corpus's Extension-workforce finding.
- **Verification posture.** Government primary document, self-reported. Longer-term claims (import diversification, export expansion) are directions, not outcomes.
- **Read the full text, not the summaries.** The published text runs to several thousand characters; consultancy summaries circulating in English (including one already cited in the corpus for a "2027 Plan") compress these articles into a different structure.

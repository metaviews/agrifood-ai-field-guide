## Content

The September 2026 China cycle established the corpus's reserve-and-platform-first reading of Chinese agri-AI and left two named leads open: the agricultural LLMs (written as `units/china-agricultural-llms-landscape.md`) and the **state farms and provincial deployment vehicles**. This unit writes the second. Its substance: **Beidahuang is the corpus's largest single agri-AI deployment vehicle anywhere** — a state farm group that owns roughly 48M mu of farmland, runs its own information company, its own satellite-analytics estate, its own service-fee platform, and a joint state key lab with HIT and NEAU — and Heilongjiang province is the first province to issue a smart-agriculture implementation plan through the provincial government office, with Beidahuang and Jiamusi designated the province's two smart-agriculture 样板区 (model zones) in 2025. The XPCC, by contrast, is the thinnest evidence in the corpus's China coverage: division-level cases exist; **no corps-level AI or smart-agriculture programme document was found** (G-479).

### Beidahuang — the data-platform estate (company newsroom, 21 April 2026, read in full)

Beidahuang Information Co. (北大荒信息有限公司), the group's smart-agriculture subsidiary, reports an estate of:

- **Platforms:** 智壤 coomap (the "one map"), 离朱 intelligent remote-sensing cloud, 耘图 large-scale planting-management platform, and the 河洛 platform in iteration — **99 algorithms** integrated, ~3,000 datasets online, crop-growth algorithms refreshing across the reclamation area within **5 days**, auto-generating plant-protection prescription maps and ~50 custom remote-sensing report types.
- **Scale:** 48,000+ GIS layer services; **19 agricultural systems** fully integrated; **48 satellites** completing reclamation-wide staple-crop monitoring in **2 hours**; yield-estimation and farm-progress monitoring accuracy improved 5-10%; pilot-farm seedling recognition **>87%**; smart-equipment platform covering **111 farms with 84,000 connected units**; sensing across **4.64M mu**; smart irrigation on 54 farms with **19,000 automated gates** (water saving 10-20%; a 1,000-mu rice block saving CNY 12,000/year in water management); smart fertilisation at −10-20% fertiliser, rice +3-5% yield, +80-100 CNY/mu income.
- **Recognition:** first-batch National Data Administration public-data application demonstration scenario; first prize, Heilongjiang 数据要素× contest (modern-agriculture track) and second prize at the national finals; first prize, national digital-village innovation contest; selected into **MARA's 2025 smart-agriculture promoted technologies**.
- **Unmanned farms** (overlapping `units/china-ground-robotics-unmanned-farms.md`, which holds the robotics detail): 万亩-scale operations with paddy/dryland labour reduced **50.3% / 51.5%**; the "北大荒未来农场" model piloted at Bawuwu and Heshan farms over 15,000 mu (Bawuwu soybean +5% yield, −5% fertiliser), and written into the provincial implementation plan as a promoted technology.
- **R&D:** 127 research projects (46 national key, 45 provincial/ministerial); seven landmark outcomes including its own crop-growth-model prescription-map technology; the Smart Farm Technology and Systems State Key Lab jointly with **HIT and Northeast Agricultural University**, co-developing 天工开悟 (`units/china-agricultural-llms-landscape.md`), the 寒地作物大模型 (cold-region crops model) and the **BDH-Rice** crop model.

### Beidahuang — the group frame and the money (provincial MARA / Heilongjiang Daily, 31 March 2026, read in full)

The group level adds the institutional and economic layers:

- **《集团智慧农业行动方案（2026-2030年）》** issued, with core tasks "build smart farms, create model zones" under a three-tier **集团统筹、产业协同、农场落地** (group coordinates / industries coordinate / farms implement) system — the group's own five-year smart-agriculture plan, sitting inside a wider **new-quality-productive-forces action plan (2024-2030)** adopted October 2024 and folded into the group's 15th FYP strategic development plan in January 2026.
- **Infrastructure:** 25,300 皮长公里 of five-level fibre network; **>90% of farms** with 四情监测 (four-situation monitoring) equipment; ~**203 TB** stored data; **270,000+ plots** each with a unique digital identity; 智壤's holographic one-map covering 21 themes with digital files for **4,800+万亩 of farmland, 600,000 planting households and 760,000+ farm machines**.
- **The 农服 App is the economic datum:** cumulative online service fees of **CNY 101.8 billion** and **250 million mu-times** of machinery dispatch. This is the corpus's first hard revenue-side figure for Chinese agricultural AI services — and it is unaudited (G-481). The platform's provincial context: 2,111.4万亩次 of agricultural socialised services delivered.
- **The machinery base beneath it:** 680,700 units of farm machinery, 14.24M kW total power, mechanisation stable above **99.7%** — the physical preconditions the corpus's catalogue-precondition analysis predicted would gate autonomous agriculture.

### Heilongjiang province — the programme frame

Heilongjiang is the **first province to issue a smart-agriculture implementation plan** (《黑龙江省大力发展智慧农业实施方案》) through the provincial government office, with a route-map and timetable; Beidahuang and Jiamusi were designated the province's two smart-agriculture model zones in 2025. Provincial work-dynamics give a fleet-level telemetry picture: **16,000 new smart plant-protection drones** in a year (provincial fleet ~33,000), 700M mu-times of aerial operations, **121,800 machinery-monitoring terminals**, 4.007M mu of drip irrigation — province-reported with no method note (V1-official figures, G-481). The same documents place Beidahuang's Weishan farm among MARA's 16 national 2025 smart-agriculture typical cases and the group's 主粮种植数字管控技术 among the ten nationally promoted technologies. A provincial page (September 2026, discovery pass, not directly read) reports **CNY 230 million cumulative** invested across the 40 pilot farms. Suihua runs a "1+2+3+10" smart-agriculture system with 32 smart farms and a national BeiDou+ smart-agriculture pilot. The 2026 provincial government work report commits to the "人工智能+" action and a national "AI+" agriculture pilot-testing (中试) base — which HIT's Liu Jie team is building toward (`units/china-agricultural-llms-landscape.md`).

### The XPCC — division-level only

No corps-level (兵团) AI or smart-agriculture programme document surfaced in this pass — a genuine negative finding (G-479). What exists is division-level and citable but scattered: the **8th Division Shihezi** cotton-production LLM on domestic GPU compute with air-ground-space sensing (兵团农业农村局-affiliated media, with a named bureau official quoted — the closest thing to an XPCC AI statement found, but the model has no named developer, name or parameter scale); the first XPCC agricultural **data product** (新疆耘筹's corn whole-cycle product registered at the Guangzhou Data Exchange, April 2025, digital services on 4M+ mu, LLM-generated "health-CT" field reports); the 10th Division Beitun "数字十师" app (2025; five middle platforms, 10+ systems); the 6th Division Qitai farm model (+30% drip uniformity, −15% cost). Adjacent but not XPCC: Changji prefecture's three-year unmanned-machinery-swarm project and Weili's 3,000-mu two-person super cotton field. Read structurally: the XPCC behaves like **14 Beidahuangs without a group-level platform company** — deployment without an integrating estate.

## New contested claims surfaced

- **C-361 (new):** "State farms are the deployment vehicle that makes Chinese agricultural AI real at scale — the estate layer, not the farmer layer." Supported by the estate's documented scale (48M+ mu digital files, 111 farms, 99 algorithms in production) and its institutional completeness (own information company, own lab, own service platform); countered by the fact that every operating figure is self-reported, the service-fee total has no independent check, and the model layer it hosts is itself unaudited (G-476).

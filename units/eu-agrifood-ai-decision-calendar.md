## Content

This unit is the tracked decision calendar for EU agrifood AI, 2026-2028 — the single corpus artefact that `scans/2026-09-eu-substrate-deepening.md` §7 named as missing when it opened **G-436**: the EU's agrifood-AI-relevant decisions are spread across eight instruments with eight decision calendars (AI Act, Machinery Regulation, CRCF, Apply AI / AI Continent Action Plan, EuroHPC procurement, Horizon Europe / MFF, the EDIC, and CAP-adjacent funding), and no actor — inside or outside the EU — publishes them as one view. **G-436 is closed substantively by this unit; the gap it named was the artefact's non-existence.**

The calendar is the evidence base for the September cycle's central findings: *there is no EU agrifood AI policy* (`talks/eu-china-cycles-2026-09.md` finding EU-1), and the EU cluster pattern is *instrument-dispersed and procedural* (finding EU-8). Read down the date column and the dispersion is visible: in a fourteen-month window (November 2026 - December 2027) at least eleven distinct decision moments land, owned by at least six distinct decision-makers, under five different legal or procurement instruments — and **not one of them is a decision to fund or build an agrifood AI programme**. The two rows that are agrifood-specific (the Apply AI "Agri-food AI platform", the EDIC work programme) are precisely the rows with no budget or no date.

**Row types.** Each row is typed, because the types carry different reliability: **statutory** (fixed in legislation; moves only by amendment — the AI Act dates moved this way in July 2026), **administrative** (procurement or Commission scheduling; demonstrably movable — the gigafactory deadline slipped twice in five weeks), **target** (an institution's own stated aim; treat as announcement until transacted), **negotiation** (outcome and date both open).

## What the calendar shows (the analytical read)

1. **Density without programme.** Eleven dated decision moments in fourteen months, six-plus decision-makers, five instruments — and zero rows that constitute an agrifood AI programme decision. The EU's agrifood AI "policy" is a calendar of adjacent instruments, which is exactly the dispersion finding in date form.
2. **The reliability gradient is the story.** The statutory rows are solid and mostly *not* agrifood-specific; the agrifood-specific rows (Apply AI platform, EDIC work programme) are targets and undated negotiations. Where the EU is concrete, it is not talking about agrifood; where it talks about agrifood, it is not concrete.
3. **Administrative dates slip; statutory dates move only by amendment.** The gigafactory call slipped twice in five weeks (12 Nov → 3 Dec 2026 → Lot 1 to 4 Feb 2027) with submitted tenders discarded; the AI Act's high-risk dates moved once, by the Digital Omnibus, and are now settled law. Talks should carry statutory dates as facts, administrative dates as "current as of", and targets as announcements.
4. **2027 is the operative year.** Machinery Regulation application (Jan), both sandbox and equivalence deadlines (Aug), Annex III (Dec), the platform target (Q1), gigafactory selection — the compliance and funding architecture firms would actually plan against is a 2027 shape, not a 2026 one.
5. **The carbon track runs on its own clock.** Methodologies adopted but not yet in force, zero schemes recognised, first removals purchases targeted Dec 2026, agricultural units H1 2028 — the CRCF sequence is the calendar's clearest case of market-creation by procedure, and every step is verifiable.

## Maintenance protocol

- **This is a living artefact, not a dated snapshot.** Rows are updated in place; each status change appends its as-of date rather than deleting the prior state (the slip history of the gigafactory row is part of the record). Dated snapshot-consolidations (`talks/eu-china-cycles-2026-09.md` and successors) cite this unit; they do not duplicate the table.
- **Trigger cadence:** check within one week after each dated row lands (nearest: 17 Nov 2026 summit outcome; then the 2-8 Dec 2026 cluster), and at minimum monthly while any row is inside its landing quarter. EUR-Lex publication of C(2026)4666 is a same-week check (G-433 closes on the OJ number appearing).
- **Row discipline:** a row leaves the calendar when its decision has landed and the outcome is recorded in the underlying unit; the landed-outcome note stays in this unit's Freshness log. New rows enter only with a primary source or a V1/V2-verified corpus unit behind them.
- Statutory dates are re-verified against EUR-Lex at each refresh; administrative dates against the owning institution's page; targets are never promoted to facts without a transaction or a legal act.

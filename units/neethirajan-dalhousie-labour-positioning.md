## Content

Suresh Neethirajan (Dalhousie University Research Chair in Digital Livestock Farming; Mooanalytica research team lead) is the substantive Canadian academic-policy critical voice on AI-and-labour. The cycle's contribution is to consolidate Neethirajan's positioning on labour specifically — the rural-urban gap, Green AI + dairy/poultry, succession, and cybersecurity-as-critical-infrastructure framings are all labour-side by construction.

This unit complements `units/neethirajan-dalhousie-ecosystem.md` (the existing Neethirajan four-layer ecosystem map) by extracting the *labour-side* dimensions of Neethirajan's analytical positioning into a load-bearing analytical anchor for Canadian AI-and-labour talks.

### The rural-urban gap as labour-side framing

Per `quotes/researchers-and-experts/neethirajan-suresh-ai-strategy-stops-at-city-limits.md`:

> *"Canada's AI strategy stops at city limits."*

The rural-urban gap is labour-side by construction:
- Canadian agricultural labour is rural.
- SAWP + TFWP workers are rural.
- Succession is rural (family-farm succession is rural; corporate-anchor labour is urban).
- The labour-side dimension of the rural-urban gap is the *who works where* question — Canadian agricultural labour concentration is rural; AI policy + AI strategy is urban.
- The Neethirajan rural-urban gap framing is the substantive Canadian academic-policy critical voice on AI-and-labour; it parallels Sullan's "zero percent" head lettuce framing as labour-side critical voice on US specialty-crop AI deployment.

### Green AI + dairy/poultry as labour-side framing

Per `quotes/researchers-and-experts/neethirajan-suresh-green-ai-cybersecurity.md`: Neethirajan's Frontiers Sustainable Food Systems April 2026 *"Green AI for climate-smart dairy and poultry"* paper makes the *Green AI + computational sustainability* framing.

The Green AI + dairy/poultry framing is labour-side by construction:
- Dairy robotics (Lely Astronaut; 50,000 robots / 3M cows milked daily / 30-year deployment; family-farm succession motivation per Torrie 2018) is the substantive dairy AI labour-displacement question.
- Poultry AI (Tyson × AWS; Cargill CarVe-equivalent; CAAIN-funded Farm Health Guardian Guelph ON × Feather Board Command Centre per Canada deepening cycle) is the substantive poultry AI labour-displacement question.
- Green AI computational sustainability is the *energy-efficiency dimension* of dairy + poultry AI deployment; the labour-side dimension is *which labour is augmented + which labour is displaced*.

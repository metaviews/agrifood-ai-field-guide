## Content

《数字乡村高质量发展行动计划（2026—2030年）》, jointly issued by the **Cyberspace Administration of China (中央网信办), MARA, and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology**, announced **11 September 2026** — three days before this unit was written. It sets the 十五五 (15th Five-Year Plan) framework for digital rural development, succeeding the Digital Villages action plan for 2022–2025.

What the release establishes:

- **Horizon and scope.** To 2030: rural information infrastructure optimised; the *integrated* level of **乡村产业数字化 (rural industry digitalisation), 乡村建设信息化 (rural construction informatisation) and 乡村治理智慧化 (rural governance intelligence)** "markedly raised"; a more complete sustainability mechanism; and farmers' "sense of gain, happiness and security" in sharing information-technology fruits.
- **The instrument set.** **Eight main development indicators to 2030** plus **five areas and twenty-four work measures**: consolidating the digital-village development base; raising the level of rural industry digitalisation; raising the level of rural construction informatisation; raising the level of rural governance intelligence; and stimulating internal momentum. Implementation requirements include strengthened policy coordination and resource pooling, a "correct view of political performance" (正确政绩观), and adaptation of measures to local conditions.
- **The lineage it claims.** Anchored in the **"千万工程" (Thousand-Village Demonstration, Ten-Thousand-Village Rectification) experience** — the rural environmental improvement programme that Chinese policy has generalised into a governing method — and in the fourth plenum deployment of the 20th Central Committee.

What the release does **not** contain: the text of the eight indicators (they are referenced, not listed), the measures, or any agriculture-specific AI provision. The release is a summary announcement; the plan document itself was not published with it.

## Why it matters for talks

- **Three-ministry joint issuance is the story.** Agricultural digitalisation in China is governed as much by the information-policy apparatus as by the agriculture ministry, which is unlike any other jurisdiction in this corpus.
- **The triad — industry, construction, governance — is the definition of "digital village."** AI is not the organising concept: rural industry digitalisation is, with governance digitalisation as a parallel arm.
- **The 25-year lineage (2022–2025 plan → 2026–2030 plan)** shows continuous, planned state programming across plan cycles, which is the structural contrast with the announcement-driven pattern in the corpus's vendor units.
- **Eight indicators exist but are not public in the release.** Worth knowing simply to avoid treating the release as the plan.

## Critical context

- **Verification posture.** The release is a summary announcement from CAC; the indicators and measures it references are not published in it. Any numeric claim about this plan's targets requires the plan document or a ministry briefing.
- **"高质量发展" (high-quality development) in the title is a downgrade signal in disguise.** The 2022–2025 plan was an expansion-era document (build the infrastructure); the 2026–2030 plan is framed around quality and sustainability mechanisms, which is consistent with the base being in place and the returns being uneven.
- **正确政绩观 ("correct view of political performance")** is included deliberately: it is the standard Chinese formulation against performative, target-chasing local projects — the same failure mode the corpus's honesty thread tracks in vendor deployment claims, here acknowledged by the issuing ministries.
- **The release is nine months into the 15th Five-Year Plan.** Reading it as the start of a new phase would be wrong; it is the digital-rural component of an already-running plan cycle.

# Taxonomy — v6 (unit-kind, governance-level, axis extensions, enforcement)

**Status.** Current. Released 15 November 2026, superseding v5 (`taxonomy/v5.md`) with the v5.1 errata (`taxonomy/v5.1.md`) folded in; both are preserved as change-history anchors. v5's three primary axes, eleven cross-cutting tags, and four maturity dimensions remain the structural core. v6 adds two tags, extends two axis vocabularies, formalizes a two-tier maturity norm, and, for the first time, makes the taxonomy machine-enforced.

**Why now.** The 2026-11-15 audit measured the pressure: 107 of 249 units (43%) carry "(cross-cutting)" in sector-position and 100 in technique class. The three-axis grid still locates deployments well, but the corpus has grown beyond deployments: policy instruments, funders, data infrastructure, meta-pattern analyses, critical voices, maintained trackers. The axes increasingly mapped where units were *not*. v6 answers that with a facet for what a unit *is*, rather than rebuilding the axes. Secondary findings: claim-type had grown ~110 de-facto composite values (informative, not noise — the v5.1 interim grammar records it); zero units tagged "agentic" despite an agentic wave inside the corpus; the China cycle's central purpose (state capacity and visibility) had no home in axis 3; and vocabulary drift (region, claim-type) was only catchable by archaeology.

---

## 1. New tag: unit-kind (all 249 units assigned)

Answers: *what sort of thing is this unit about?* This is the navigation facet the cross-cutting majority needs — a unit can span every cell of the sector/technique grid and still be findable by kind.

| Value | Definition | Count |
|---|---|---|
| deployment | A product, system, platform, or estate in the world: vendor offerings, adopter deployments, consolidated vendor-cells (including discontinued ones — `activity-status` carries the status, kind carries the nature) | 114 |
| institution | An organization as the subject: labs, institutes, universities, associations, state agencies as bodies, community organizations | 33 |
| policy-instrument | A law, regulation, plan, strategy, standard, catalogue, or normative framework (state or governance-body), and units centred on a regulatory regime or its absence | 29 |
| ecosystem-map | A landscape, cluster, substrate, convening, or progress report: units whose subject is a mapped field rather than a single actor or instrument | 20 |
| critical-analysis | A critical voice, position paper, counter-narrative, or harm analysis as the unit's centre of gravity | 16 |
| funding-programme | A funder, fund, grant programme, or funded-portfolio as the subject | 12 |
| meta-pattern | A cross-case pattern observation: regional patterns, cluster patterns, cross-border comparisons, labour-context syntheses | 9 |
| data-infrastructure | A data platform, commons, identifier system, open dataset, or interoperability layer as the subject | 9 |
| methodology | A framework, method, or analytical instrument the corpus itself uses or documents (maturity framework, breeding methodology, correlation notes, quantitative panels) | 6 |
| tracker-artefact | A maintained, date-typed artefact updated on triggers (currently: the EU decision calendar) | 1 |

**Assignment method.** Rule-based batch classification from existing frontmatter signals (id, claim-type, actor-type, activity-status, policy-instrument), then full-list human review: every one of the 249 assignments was read and 60 corrected via explicit override tables (kept in the migration script's commit history rationale; the corrections clustered around vendor-cell consolidations wrongly promoted to meta-pattern, Indigenous community deployments wrongly read as institutions, and "law" substring false-positives). Distribution verified after correction.

**Maintenance rule.** New units get `unit-kind` at creation (the validator requires it). Re-kind a unit only when its centre of gravity changes, and say so in the unit's log.

## 2. New tag: governance-level (governmental primary actors only)

Answers: *at what level of governance does this unit's primary actor operate?* The EU cycle forced this: Flanders-in-EDIC (subnational), WAICO (multilateral), EU instruments (supranational) were all level-distinctions buried in prose, and the cluster-pattern confirmation test for the EU candidate is literally a question about level.

| Value | Definition | Count |
|---|---|---|
| subnational | Province, state, region, emirate-level actor or programme (e.g. provincial state-farm deployment, ADAFSA) | 2 |
| national | Federal/national ministries, agencies, Crown corporations, national programmes | 34 |
| supranational | EU-level institutions and instruments | 12 |
| multilateral | Treaty-based and international organizations (IICA, WAICO, OECD, CGIAR centres, FAO-facing bodies) | 10 |

**Assignment principle.** The tag reflects the *first-listed* actor-type in `actor-type:`; composite or non-governmental primaries omit the tag entirely (191 units). Location mentions do not set level: a national programme deployed in provinces stays `national`; only a provincially-governed primary actor is `subnational`. Non-governmental units carry no tag rather than "n/a" — absence means the question doesn't apply.

## 3. Axis extensions

**Axis 2 (technique class) adds: `agentic systems / harness layer`.** The corpus's newest technical wave had no home: Shennong's 36 specialist agents and Tiangong Kaiwu's 1,200-algorithm platform (china-agricultural-llms-landscape), Sinong's multi-agent RAG, Cropin OrbitAI on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform exposed as an MCP server (cropin-india), and Mozilla's finding that the fight has moved from the model to the harness. Without the value, all of this filed under plain "generative AI / LLMs" and the axis lost resolution exactly where the field is moving. Retagged: china-agricultural-llms-landscape, cropin-india.

**Axis 3 (purpose) adds: `state capacity and visibility`.** The China cycle's central finding does not fit any v5 purpose: instrumenting what the state owns — grain reserves, state-farm land, subsidy flows, the national data estate — for administrative legibility and control. "Governance" as v5 defines it (data governance, regulatory traceability) is not this. Seven units already used control/visibility language off-vocabulary. Retagged: china-grain-storage-ai-jiyuan, china-state-farms-provincial-deployment, china-mara-agricultural-data-resources-2026.

## 4. Conventions folded in from v5.1

- **Region grammar** (`PREFIX (free-text detail)`, controlled prefix list, comma-segment multi-region lists, `(cross-cutting — reason)` for non-geographic units) — now enforced by the validator.
- **Claim-type interim grammar** `PRIMARY (qualifier) [+ SECONDARY (qualifier)]`. The recognized primaries live in `scripts/schemas/v6.json`. Migration direction: now that `unit-kind` carries kind semantics structurally, claim-type should return to epistemic primaries (example / claim / statistic / framework, plus fact for trackers) **at refresh cadence only** — the composite values carry sourced qualifiers worth keeping until a unit is next opened.

## 5. Maturity tags: the two-tier norm

Coverage stands at 123/249 graded (after the 15 November 2026 transcription of the framework table's July grades). This is formalized as a **two-tier state, not an incomplete migration**: `graded` (all four maturity-* tags present) and `ungraded`. Both are queryable and honest. Grading completes at refresh cadence, never in bulk without re-reading sources — a grade is an assertion about evidence quality, and stale assertion is worse than declared absence. v5 §7's watch item (maturity as a fourth primary axis) stays deferred; the two-tier state is what the site can already surface (see §7).

## 6. Enforcement: vocabulary as data + validator + CI

The taxonomy stops being only a document librarians read:

- **`scripts/schemas/v6.json`** — the vocabularies (unit-kind, governance-level, claim-type primaries, region prefixes, required fields, maturity pattern) live as data. Change the vocabulary here first, then the taxonomy doc.
- **`scripts/validate_frontmatter.py`** — stdlib-only validator (parity with build_gap_lead_registries.py). Checks required fields, unit-kind/governance-level vocabulary membership, claim-type composite grammar (paren-depth-aware splitting), region leading tokens, maturity ladder patterns, and the YAML hazard class that broke the site twice (unquoted `: ` in values, quote-escaping errors). Exit 1 on any error. Acceptance state at release: 249/249 units, 0 errors.
- **`.github/workflows/validate-corpus.yml`** — runs the validator and the registry drift check on every push/PR touching units/, quotes/, scripts/, taxonomy/. Drift like the August deployment break and the November region sprawl now fails a build instead of awaiting archaeology.

## 7. Deferred by design (next structural steps)

- **Typed relations** (corrects / supersedes / instance-of-pattern / comparator / tracks): the corpus's correction culture is its most distinctive epistemic feature and still lives in prose. Formalizing it needs unit-kind stable first (done now) and should land when the next refresh cycle produces corrections worth graphing. Bounded backfill: ~15 documented correction pairs.
- **Faceted navigation on the site**: Pagefind supports metadata filters with no server. Once wired, the site can offer facets on unit-kind, region, and verification tier — and "show me everything that is vendor-reported only" (V0) is a query no other agrifood-AI resource can answer. The zod content schema now carries unit-kind and governance-level so the data reaches the content layer.

## 8. Frontmatter template (v6)

```yaml
---
id: slug-name
title: 'Human-readable title'
sector-position: <axis 1 value or (cross-cutting — reason)>
ai-technique-class: <axis 2 value(s), semicolon-separated; may include agentic systems / harness layer>
purpose: <axis 3 value(s); may include state capacity and visibility>
claim-type: PRIMARY (qualifier) [+ SECONDARY (qualifier)]
unit-kind: <one of the 10 values in §1>          # required, v6
governance-level: <subnational|national|supranational|multilateral>   # only for governmental primary actors, v6
activity-status: <status with date>
critical-voice: <voice or (none documented)>
capital-intensity: <profile>
language-literacy-profile: <profile>
policy-instrument: <instrument or none>
region: PREFIX (free-text detail)                 # v5.1 grammar, enforced
actor: '<named actors>'
actor-type: '<first-listed type sets governance-level>'
data-governance: <regime>
data-rights-framework: <framework or none>
maturity-scale: S0-S4 | Sx-Sy | (ungraded reason)  # two-tier norm, §5
maturity-verification: V0-V4 | range
maturity-longevity: L0-L4 | range
maturity-translation: T0-T4 | range
last-verified: YYYY-MM
---
```

1. **Canada has substantial labour-relevant substrate already — 9 distinct Canadian units cover labour-side content directly, plus 4 Canadian scans + 2 Canadian scouts + the constraint/critical scout name the labour-organisation layer explicitly.** The cycle's contribution is *consolidation* + *Canadian-specific depth* + *labour-side institutional voice material* that the global cycle could not exhaust at global scope.

3. **Neethirajan (Dalhousie; Policy Options January 2026 *"Canada's AI strategy stops at city limits"*; Frontiers Sustainable Food Systems April 2026 *"Green AI for climate-smart dairy and poultry"*) is the substantive Canadian academic-policy critical voice.** Neethirajan's *Canada's AI strategy stops at city limits* explicitly engages the rural-urban gap, which is the labour-side rural-workforce dimension. **Neethirajan's existing `neethirajan-dalhousie-ecosystem` unit + `ai-strategy-stops-at-city-limits` paraphrased-position quote are the anchor; the cycle's Canadian-labour contribution is to consolidate Neethirajan's positioning on labour specifically.**

4. **Canadian NA processing labour-displacement is *substantial* but read-together-thin across existing units.** Maple Leaf Foods Braincube + Emulate3D (digital twin + plant-optimisation; +12% yield / 3-month ROI; London ON poultry plant "world's largest"); SoraLINK × Saputo/Olymel/Agropur (predictive maintenance; $250K saved per intervention; Saputo + Olymel + Agropur are UFCW-organised); Cargill CarVe (Fort Morgan CO $90M; named in the global cycle's NA meat-processing unit); Tyson × AWS poultry CV (US-side cross-deployment); Smithfield vision + robotics (US-side; WH Group China parent since 2013). **The Canadian-side processing-cell is multi-protein + multi-deployment; reading them together against UFCW Canada representation is the substantive Canadian labour-displacement consolidation move.**

5. **AIDA (Artificial Intelligence and Data Act, Bill C-27) died on the order paper at prorogation on 6 January 2025. CPPA (Consumer Privacy Protection Act) died with it.
